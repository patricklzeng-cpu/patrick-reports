L2 SYNTHESIS

01 · 任务比岗位更适合作为分析单位01 · Tasks are the better unit of analysis

AI 先压缩岗位中的可编码任务，再改变剩余任务的相对价值。岗位不会整齐地“消失”，但入口职位与以替代为主的流程会先承压。AI first compresses codifiable tasks, changing the value of what remains. Jobs do not vanish cleanly, but entry roles and substitution-heavy workflows absorb pressure first.

L2 SYNTHESIS

02 · 上下文是人的运营护城河02 · Context is the human operating moat

客户表情、组织约束、历史反馈与隐性偏好构成模型看不到的状态。优势不只是“有品味”，而是持续采集、压缩并调用这些上下文。Customer reactions, organizational constraints, historical feedback, and tacit preferences form state the model cannot see. The advantage is not vague taste; it is the system for capturing and applying context.

L2 SYNTHESIS

03 · “会构建”正在成为通用素养03 · Building is becoming general literacy

营销、招聘、财务与运营人员的门槛从“会用聊天工具”移到“能交付可重复运行的制品”。代码更便宜，跨职能原型的价值反而上升。The bar moves from using a chat tool to shipping a repeatable artifact. As code gets cheaper, cross-functional prototyping becomes more valuable.

L2 SYNTHESIS

04 · AI KPI 必须回到业务结果04 · AI metrics must resolve to business outcomes

提示词数量、调用次数与“采用率”不是终点。增长、留存、响应速度、准确率、风险与单位成本才是能决定去留的指标。Prompt counts, calls, and adoption rates are not outcomes. Growth, retention, service speed, accuracy, risk, and unit cost determine whether an AI workflow deserves to stay.

L2 SYNTHESIS

05 · 绩效提升与学习发生分叉05 · Performance and learning can diverge

AI 能提高当下作业表现，同时削弱无辅助回忆。生产模式与学习模式需要不同交互：前者追求完成，后者要求先尝试、只给提示、延迟答案并做回忆测试。AI can improve assisted performance while weakening unaided recall. Production mode optimizes completion; learning mode should require an attempt, offer hints, delay answers, and test retrieval.

L2 SYNTHESIS

06 · 瓶颈从开发转向产品判断06 · The bottleneck shifts to product judgment

几小时原型不等于可持续业务。更稀缺的是选问题、听客户、处理复杂性、建立分发与在反馈中持续聚焦。A prototype built in hours is not a durable company. Scarcity moves to problem selection, customer listening, integration depth, distribution, and sustained focus.

L2 SYNTHESIS

07 · 拒绝总括性恐惧，也拒绝总括性安全感07 · Reject blanket fear and blanket reassurance

深度伪造、敏感数据、认知外包与青年招聘是可观测风险；“岗位末日”与抽象失控叙事则需要更高证据门槛。可操作立场是逐风险分类，而不是在乐观与悲观之间站队。Deepfakes, sensitive data, cognitive offloading, and early-career hiring are observable risks. Job-apocalypse and abstract loss-of-control stories demand stronger evidence. The operational stance is risk-by-risk classification, not optimism versus pessimism.

CHAPTER 04 · LEARNING SCIENCE

AI 可以提高表现，却降低学习AI can raise performance while reducing learning

11:59 ↗

关键区分是“在辅助下做得更好”与“撤掉辅助后仍然会”。如果系统过早给出完整答案，短期产出会上升，提取练习、错误诊断和概念组织却可能减少。The critical distinction is doing better with assistance versus retaining the ability without it. OFFICIAL CONTEXT

The defensible conclusion is design-dependent—not that AI is uniformly harmful or beneficial.
