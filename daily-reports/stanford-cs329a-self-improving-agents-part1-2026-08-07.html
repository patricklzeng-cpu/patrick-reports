EXECUTIVE THESIS

Self-improvement 不是魔法属性，而是可测量的反馈闭环。Self-improvement is not a magical trait; it is a measurable feedback loop.

把目标写清、把任务分解、让工具产生外部证据、用 verifier 判断、允许纠错并定义停止条件。模型只是环中的一个部件。Specify the goal, decompose the task, produce external evidence through tools, verify it, permit correction, and define stopping conditions. The model is one component in the loop.
