一句话核心论点One-line core thesis

AI 不在"取代工作"——它把每个职业拆成 N 个任务，把执行自动化，让人更聚焦定义问题 + 评估结果。25 岁以下 + AI 暴露 = 已经裁 16%；但同时最低暴露职业（家庭护理、健康助理）就业增长。这不是 0-1 取代，是 1-N 重组。胜负手 = 你会不会用 AI 放大自己而不是被它替代。AI isn't "replacing jobs" — it splits every occupation into N tasks, automates execution, and makes humans focus on defining the problem + evaluating the result. Under-25 in AI-exposed jobs already lost 16% — but the least exposed occupations (home health, aides) are growing. This is not 0/1 replacement, it's 1/N reshuffling. The winner is whoever uses AI to augment themselves, not gets replaced by it.

1. 任务 ≠ 职业1. Tasks ≠ Occupations 基础

"每个职业是一束任务"——放射科医生 = 26 个任务（读片 / 体检 / 协调 / 审核化验）。Hinton 2017 错判了"AI 能读片 = 取代放射科医生"。真相反而是：AI 取代 1 个任务 → 解放人类做其余 25 个 → 需求更旺 → 人员更多。"Every occupation is a bundle of tasks" — radiologist = 26 tasks (read images / physical exam / coordinate / review labs). Hinton's 2017 prediction "AI can read images = replacing radiologists" was wrong. Reality: AI replaces 1 task → frees humans for the other 25 → demand rises → more people, not fewer.

3. 弹性决定胜负3. Elasticity decides winners 经济

不是看 AI 会不会"取代"，而是看需求曲线弹性——AI 降低价格后，需求量上升多少？Not whether AI will "replace" — but rather the demand curve elasticity: how much does demand rise after AI drops the price?

  • 陡峭（inelastic）：Steep (inelastic): AI 降本 → 你的收入下降（如编码 / 文书）cost down → your income drops (coding / paperwork)
  • 平缓（elastic）：Flat (elastic): AI 降本 → 需求爆炸 → 你的收入上升（医疗 / 教育 / 服务）cost down → demand explodes → income rises (medical / education / services)
  • 约一半经济是平缓的（医疗 + 教育 + 体验）~Half the economy is elastic (medical + education + experiences)

AI: DeepMind tells Brynjolfsson "all capabilities online in 18–24 months" — but capability online ≠ business value landing. J-curve: capabilities rise steeply, but business value takes 3–5 years to show up in GDP.

真正的"金丝雀"数据The Real "Canaries" Data

所有职业里，LLM 都不能 100% 接管任何一个。每个职业都有"AI 暴露核心 + 人类专属周边"的混合结构。后果：In every occupation, LLM cannot 100% take over any single one. Every job has a "AI-exposed core + human-only periphery" mix. The consequence:

  • AI 提升 1 个任务效率 → 整个职业效率提升AI raises 1 task's efficiency → whole occupation's productivity rises
  • 效率提升 → 价格下降Productivity rise → price drop
  • 价格下降 → 需求上升（弹性高时）Price drop → demand rises (if elastic)
  • 需求上升 → 招聘更多Demand rises → hire more

结果：放射科医生 人手短缺 + 工资不降，Hinton 被打脸。Result: radiologists shortage + wages stable, Hinton proven wrong.
