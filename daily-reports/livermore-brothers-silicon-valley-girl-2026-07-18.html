05 · 6 个失败模式(讲师本人讲的) 05 · 6 Failure Modes (per the speakers)

FAILURE 1

温水侵蚀型集中化Slow-boiled centralization

不是一刀切夺走自由,而是一个产业一个产业被吸收:超市→在线配送→机器人。短期新岗位上升,长期下滑。Not abrupt coup, but industry-by-industry absorption: grocery→delivery→robot. Short-term job spike, long-term decline.

FAILURE 2

伪开放陷阱(中国模式)Fake-open trap (China model)

"open weight ≠ open source"。服务器在中国 + 数据中心在中国 + 芯片在中国,等于不在你控制之下。"Open weight ≠ open source." Servers in China + data centers in China + chips in China = not under your control.

FAILURE 3

生态锁定陷阱Ecosystem lock-in trap

当 chat + calendar + email + docs + image + music + shopping 全集成进一个生态,切换成本爆炸。Cursor / Figma 正被 Frontier 模型吸收。When chat + calendar + email + docs + image + music + shopping all integrate into one ecosystem, switching cost explodes. Cursor / Figma absorbed by Frontier.

FAILURE 4

信源单一化Single source of truth

当 80% 的人都从同一个 AI 拿答案,这个 AI 的偏见/错误直接塑造社会共识。"信源控制 = 现实控制"。When 80% of people get answers from the same AI, that AI's bias/error shapes social consensus. "Source control = reality control."

FAILURE 5

监管滥用Regulation abuse

AI 公司老板主动要求监管——表面"防止危害",实际"锁定市场"。联邦层面监管权力会被滥用。AI CEOs actively request regulation — surface "prevent harm", actual "lock market". Federal-level regulation power will be abused.

FAILURE 6

消费者力量消解Consumer power dissolution

现在消费者能"取消订阅"施压,2 年后生态锁定消解这种力量。窗口期 = 2-3 年,过了就没了。Today consumers can "cancel subs" for pressure; in 2y ecosystem lock-in dissolves this. Window = 2-3y, then gone.
