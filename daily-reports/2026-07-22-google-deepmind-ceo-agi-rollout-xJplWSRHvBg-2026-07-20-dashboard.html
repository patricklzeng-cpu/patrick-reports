框架的 Stage 1 设立一个「标准机构」，定义能力门槛。一旦模型超过门槛 = 被认定为「前沿模型」，开发机构成为「前沿实验室」。分类依赖于能力、不是公司身份。 The framework's Stage 1 creates a 'standards body' that defines a capability threshold. Once model crosses threshold = designated 'frontier model' and the dev org becomes 'frontier lab.' Classification depends on capability NOT company identity.
