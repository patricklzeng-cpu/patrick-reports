AI and quantum computers will be frenemies

AI 与量子计算机，亦敌亦友

The technologies are not racing toward the same finish line. AI is learning how to build quantum machines; quantum must still prove where it can make AI meaningfully better.

两项技术并非冲向同一条终点线。AI 正在学习如何造好量子机器；量子计算则仍需证明，它究竟能在哪些地方真正增强 AI。

THE 90-SECOND CASE
90秒结论

One thesis.
Three time horizons.

一个命题，
三种时间尺度。

NOW / 现在

AI is already useful to quantum.

It decodes errors, tunes control systems and searches for better circuits on real hardware.

AI 已经对量子计算有用。

它正在真实硬件上解码错误、调校控制系统，并搜索更好的量子电路。

NEXT / 接下来

Quantum can help AI—selectively.

The best evidence concerns quantum data, physical experiments and highly structured optimisation.

量子计算能帮助 AI——但有选择。

最有力的证据集中在量子数据、物理实验和高度结构化的优化问题。

TENSION / 张力

The rivalry is mostly outside the machine.

Capital, talent, policy attention and ever-improving classical baselines are the real battlegrounds.

竞争主要发生在机器之外。

资本、人才、政策注意力，以及不断变强的经典算法基线，才是真正的战场。

ONE FUTURE, TWO CLOCKS
同一未来，两只时钟

Do not confuse a product cycle with a physics programme.

不要把产品周期，误认为物理学工程周期。

AI CLOCKDEPLOYED

Useful now. Scaling through chips, data, software and deployment.

已经实用。通过芯片、数据、软件与部署继续扩张。

QUANTUM CLOCKEXPERIMENTAL

Useful in narrow experiments. Scaling depends on control, error correction and proof of end-to-end advantage.

已在狭窄实验中有用。扩展取决于控制、纠错，以及端到端优势的证明。

Therefore: “Which one wins?” is the wrong question. The nearer-term question is which parts of the quantum stack AI can improve. The longer-term question is which AI workloads genuinely deserve a QPU.

所以：“谁会赢？”问错了。近端问题是 AI 能改善量子技术栈的哪些环节；远端问题是哪些 AI 负载真正值得交给 QPU。

01 · CONTROL

Keep a fragile machine on tune.

An RL controller on Google’s Willow managed more than 1,000 parameters, improved logical stability 3.5× under injected drift and added roughly 20% error suppression after expert calibration. 03

让脆弱的机器持续“在调”。

Google Willow 上的强化学习控制器管理了 1,000 多个参数；在注入漂移时，逻辑稳定性提高 3.5 倍，并在专家校准后继续带来约 20% 的错误抑制。03

CAVEAT / 限制

The larger distance-15 system and about 40,000 parameters were simulated, not demonstrated on hardware.

更大的 distance-15 系统和约 40,000 个参数只在模拟中验证，并非硬件演示。

02 · DECODING

Read noise humans cannot fully model.

AlphaQubit’s recurrent transformer learned realistic correlations, leakage and readout behaviour. It beat leading tensor-network and matching decoders on Sycamore data. 04

读懂人类难以完整建模的噪声。

AlphaQubit 的循环 Transformer 学会了真实的相关性、泄漏与读出行为，并在 Sycamore 数据上超过领先的张量网络和匹配解码器。04

CAVEAT / 限制

Accuracy is not enough: the reported decoder was still slower than the roughly 1 μs target for superconducting error-correction rounds.

准确率并非全部：论文中的解码器仍慢于超导纠错轮次约 1 微秒的目标。

03 · CIRCUITS

Search designs too awkward for hand tuning.

For a 15-qubit molecular-simulation task, AlphaEvolve reduced mean circuit-approximation error from 10.4% to 0.82% under the same hardware gate budget. 05

搜索难以手工调优的电路设计。

在一个 15 量子比特的分子模拟任务中，AlphaEvolve 在相同硬件门预算下，把平均电路近似误差从 10.4% 降至 0.82%。05

CAVEAT / 限制

Candidate scoring used a fully precomputed classical reference—an evaluation method that does not scale to genuinely beyond-classical systems.

候选评分使用了完整预计算的经典参考；到了真正超越经典能力的系统，这种评估方法无法直接扩展。

01

Learn nature from quantum data.

Quantum learners can preserve and jointly process quantum states before measurement destroys their information. Experiments have shown huge sample savings on specialised physical processes. 0607

从量子数据中学习自然。

量子学习器能在测量破坏信息前保存并联合处理量子态。实验已在特定物理过程中展示出巨大的样本节省。0607

Not the same as accelerating a spreadsheet, image set or language corpus.

这不等于加速电子表格、图像集或语言语料库。

PROMISING · STRUCTURED
02

Optimise where algebra gives leverage.

Decoded quantum interferometry points to large theoretical gains for structured optimisation. But “structured” is doing the work: tailored classical solvers remain formidable. 08

在代数结构可利用处优化。

解码量子干涉为结构化优化展示了巨大的理论收益；但“结构化”是关键词，定制经典求解器依然强大。08

Not a universal accelerator for every hard scheduling or planning problem.

它不是所有困难排程或规划问题的通用加速器。

UNPROVEN · GENERAL
03

Train mainstream AI faster.

The seductive claim remains unproven end to end. Data loading, repeated measurements, gradient cost, noise and stronger classical baselines can consume the apparent speed-up. 091011

更快训练主流 AI。

这一诱人主张尚未获得端到端证明。数据装载、重复测量、梯度成本、噪声，以及更强的经典基线，都可能吞噬表面上的加速。091011

If someone says “quantum will make LLMs exponentially faster”, ask: which algorithm, data interface, hardware and classical baseline?

若有人说“量子会让大模型指数级提速”，请追问：哪种算法、哪种数据接口、哪套硬件、对比哪个经典基线？

WHERE THEY COMPETE
真正的竞争地带

The “enemy” lives in budgets, benchmarks and belief.

“敌意”存在于预算、基准与信念之中。

01

Scarcity

Frontier researchers, patient capital, government attention and corporate experimentation budgets are finite. AI’s immediate returns can crowd out quantum’s longer horizon.

稀缺性

顶尖研究人才、耐心资本、政府注意力和企业试验预算都有限。AI 的即时回报可能挤压量子的长期投入。

02

Benchmarks

AI and better classical algorithms are not passive baselines. They learn, approximate and “dequantise” results—raising the bar every time quantum claims an advantage. 09

基准线

AI 与更好的经典算法不是静止参照物。它们会学习、近似，甚至“去量子化”成果；量子每次宣称优势，门槛都会被抬高。09

03

Credibility

Both fields sell futures. Overclaiming a distant quantum benefit by borrowing AI’s current momentum can damage the credibility needed for long-term engineering.

可信度

两者都在出售“未来”。若借 AI 当下的势头夸大量子的遥远收益，反而会损害长期工程所需的可信度。

Ignore the qubit beauty contest. Watch the interfaces.

别只看量子比特选美，去看系统接口。

  1. 01

    Logical error at useful scale

    Not the largest device—the machine that can run a deep, economically relevant circuit reliably.

    有用规模下的逻辑错误率

    重要的不是最大设备，而是能可靠运行深层、具经济意义电路的机器。

  2. 02

    Decoder latency, not just accuracy

    An excellent AI decoder that misses the hardware’s control cycle is not yet operational infrastructure.

    不仅看解码准确率，也看延迟

    再准确的 AI 解码器，若赶不上硬件控制周期，也还不是可用基础设施。

  3. 03

    End-to-end advantage

    Count loading, orchestration, measurements, error correction, energy and cost—not only the quantum kernel.

    端到端优势

    把装载、编排、测量、纠错、能耗与成本都算进去，而非只看量子内核。

  4. 04

    Strong classical baselines

    A credible quantum result must beat the best tuned classical method, not a convenient straw man.

    强经典基线

    可信的量子结果必须击败最强、充分调优的经典方法，而非方便的稻草人。

  5. 05

    Hybrid production workflows

    The inflection point is a repeatable CPU–GPU–QPU workflow tied to a real scientific or commercial decision.

    混合式生产工作流

    真正的拐点，是与真实科学或商业决策相连、可重复的 CPU–GPU–QPU 工作流。

CPUORCHESTRATE+GPULEARN+QPUACCELERATE

The likely future is not “quantum replaces AI”. It is a scheduler sending each subproblem to the processor whose physics and economics fit it best. 1213

更可能的未来并非“量子替代 AI”，而是调度器把每个子问题交给物理特性与经济性最匹配的处理器。1213

08 · OUR VERDICT / 我们的结论

Complementary.
Asymmetric.
Conditional.

互补。
不对称。
有条件。

The Economist’s provocation survives scrutiny—with an important asymmetry. AI is becoming a control layer, design partner and error interpreter for quantum hardware today. Quantum computing’s return contribution to AI is credible in quantum-native learning and selected structured problems, but not yet in ordinary large-model training.

So the two are frenemies chiefly because classical AI keeps raising the standard quantum must beat, while also helping quantum reach it.

《经济学人》的命题经得起审视，但必须加上“不对称”这个限定。今天，AI 正成为量子硬件的控制层、设计伙伴和错误解释器；量子计算对 AI 的回报，在量子原生学习与少数结构化问题中可信，却尚未延伸到普通大模型训练。

因此，它们之所以“亦敌亦友”，关键在于经典 AI 一边不断抬高量子必须跨越的门槛，一边又帮助量子跨过去。

WHAT WOULD CHANGE OUR MIND? / 什么会改变我们的判断？

A reproducible, end-to-end quantum advantage on a useful classical-data AI workload, against the best tuned classical alternative, including data movement, error correction, latency, energy and cost.

在实用的经典数据 AI 负载上，针对最强且充分调优的经典方案，展示可复现的端到端量子优势，并完整计入数据移动、纠错、延迟、能耗与成本。
