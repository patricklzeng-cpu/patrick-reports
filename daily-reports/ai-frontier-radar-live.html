06 / FIELD GUIDE

八个词，看懂前沿讨论EIGHT TERMS TO READ THE FRONTIER.

01

LLM

语言模型Language model从大量文本与代码中学习模式，核心输出是下一段 token。Learns patterns from text and code; its core output is the next token sequence.
02

VLM

视觉语言模型Vision-language model同时理解图像/视频和语言，但通常不直接控制机器。Understands images/video with language but usually does not directly control machines.
03

VLA

视觉-语言-动作模型Vision-language-action model把看到的场景和语言目标直接变成机器人动作。Turns perceived scenes and language goals directly into robot actions.
04

AGENT

智能体Agent让模型反复观察、计划、调用工具并根据结果继续行动的系统。A system that repeatedly observes, plans, calls tools, and acts on results.
05

WORLD MODEL

世界模型World model学习空间、时间和动作后果；可能是渲染器、模拟器或规划器。Learns space, time, and action consequences; may be a renderer, simulator, or planner.
06

OPEN WEIGHTS

开放权重Open weights可下载模型参数；不代表训练数据、代码和配方也开放。Downloadable parameters; it does not mean training data, code, or recipes are open.
07

CONTEXT

上下文窗口Context window一次能读入的信息上限；容量大不等于长期记忆可靠。How much can be read at once; more capacity is not reliable long-term memory.
08

MoE

混合专家Mixture of experts每个 token 只激活部分参数，以较低计算运行更大的模型。Activates only part of a model per token, running larger systems with less compute.
