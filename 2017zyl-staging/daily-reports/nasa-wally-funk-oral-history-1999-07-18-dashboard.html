5 步方法 (来源: NASA History Office 公开说明)5-step methodology (from NASA History Office public docs)

  1. 采访人 (多为 NASA 签约历史学家) 在 NASA Johnson Space Center 或受访者所在地进行 1-2 小时面对面访谈。 录音 + 笔记。Interviewer (typically NASA-commissioned historians) conducts 1-2 hour in-person interview at NASA JSC or subject's location. Audio recorded + notes.
  2. 录音由专业转录员逐字转为 transcript, 保留"嗯""呃"等口语痕迹, 不编辑内容。Audio transcribed verbatim by professional transcriptionist, preserving "um"/"uh" speech artifacts; no editorial changes.
  3. 受访者拿到 draft transcript, 增删/修改/补全。 这轮可能耗时 1-12 个月。Subject receives draft transcript, adds/removes/edits/completes. This round can take 1-12 months.
  4. 受访者签 release 协议, 同意公开。 受访者保留撤销权 (但 NASA History Office 公开记录里几乎无人撤销)。Subject signs release agreement, authorizing public release. Subject retains revocation right (but no recorded revocation in NASA History Office public records).
  5. PDF 上线 NASA History Office 网站, 标记为公共域, 任何人都可下载/翻译/引用, 无版权限制。PDF goes live on NASA History Office website, marked public domain — anyone may download/translate/cite with no copyright restriction.
