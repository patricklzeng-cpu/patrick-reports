01 / EXECUTIVE BRIEF

核心不是“要不要辞职”This is not merely “Should I quit?”

这是关于能动性、取舍与工作的设计框架。辞职只是其中一种可能动作。It is a framework for agency, trade-offs, and work design. Leaving a job is only one possible move.

一句话结论：先定义你要改变谁、改变什么，再判断眼前是可解决的问题、必须接受的处境、值得穿越的低谷，还是应当离开的死胡同。
One-line conclusion: define whom you seek to change and how, then decide whether the present is a solvable problem, a situation to accept, a Dip worth crossing, or a dead end to leave.
1

问题 ≠ 处境Problem ≠ situation

问题有可行动的改变路径；处境是目前无法由你单方面改变的事实。把处境当问题，会制造无穷摩擦。A problem has an actionable path to change; a situation is a fact you cannot presently alter alone. Treating a situation as a problem creates endless friction.

2

“但是”藏着结The knot hides inside “but”

“我想要 X，但是又要 Y”往往意味着两个目标不能同时最大化。不是更努力，而是先选优先级。“I want X, but I also need Y” often means two goals cannot be maximized together. The first move is priority, not more effort.

3

目的需要选择Purpose is chosen

工作的目的不是等待发现的神秘答案，而是你愿意承担的承诺：为谁，带来什么变化。The purpose of work is not a mystical answer waiting to be found; it is a commitment you choose: for whom, and what change.

4

坚持需要证据Persistence needs evidence

低谷有出口、里程碑与累积效应；死胡同只重复消耗。沉没成本不能证明下一步仍值得。A Dip has an exit, milestones, and compounding; a dead end only repeats cost. Sunk cost is not evidence that the next step is worthwhile.

5

练投，不练接Practice the throw, not the catch

结果依赖别人，投入更可控。把练习定义为可重复的输出、清晰规格和稳定承诺。Outcomes depend on others; inputs are more controllable. Define practice as repeatable output, a clear spec, and consistent promises.

AI

可描述的工作会被压缩Describable work gets compressed

AI 会吞掉可完整写成指令的任务；人的位置更靠近品味、信任、关系、判断和带领他人。AI absorbs tasks that can be fully described as instructions. Human leverage moves toward taste, trust, relationships, judgment, and leadership.

PARAPHRASE
不要把“好好退出”误读成“立刻辞职”。Do not misread “quitting well” as “resign immediately.”

访谈更接近一套诊断法：把结说清楚，收集低谷或死胡同的证据，先做可逆的小实验，再决定继续、重设或退出。The interview is closer to a diagnostic method: name the knot, gather Dip-or-dead-end evidence, run a reversible small experiment, then continue, redesign, or leave.

用 5 分钟把隐藏的取舍变成可见文字，然后去“下一步”页完成 14 天优先级选择。Spend five minutes making the hidden trade-off visible, then use the Actions tab to choose a 14-day priority.

02 / THE KNOT

先诊断“结”，再增加努力Diagnose the knot before adding effort

卡住经常是目标结构的问题，而不是动力不足。Stuckness is often a goal-structure problem, not a motivation shortage.

07 / AI & HUMAN EDGE

让 AI 为你工作，把人放回判断位置Make AI work for you; return humans to judgment

如果任务能被完整描述，它会快速商品化；人的优势转向难以压缩的关系与选择。If a task can be fully described, it will commoditize quickly. Human advantage moves toward relationships and choices that resist compression.

01

交给 AI：可描述的重复劳动Delegate to AI: describable repetition

整理、转换、初稿、对照、结构化检查。前提是输入边界、验收标准和风险等级都明确。Sorting, transforming, drafting, comparing, and structured checks—when input boundaries, acceptance criteria, and risk level are explicit.

02

由人验证：品味、语境与后果Human validation: taste, context, consequence

决定何为“对”、谁会受影响、错误能否逆转，以及输出是否兑现关系中的承诺。Decide what “right” means, who is affected, whether error is reversible, and whether the output keeps a relational promise.

03

人来带领：信任、勇气与方向Humans lead: trust, courage, direction

选择值得解决的问题，承担取舍，对不确定性负责，并让他人愿意一起行动。Choose worthwhile problems, own trade-offs, take responsibility under uncertainty, and help others act together.

更稳妥的策略是：用 AI 扩大投掷次数，同时把人的时间投入到受众理解、质量规格、验证区、信任和领导。A sturdier strategy: use AI to increase throws while investing human time in audience understanding, quality specs, validation zones, trust, and leadership.
