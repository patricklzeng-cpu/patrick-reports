02 · 4 个独立但纠缠的技术异常02 · 4 independent but intertwined hardware anomaliesSECTION 4

PIT 把 CFT 期间观察到的 4 个硬件异常拆开分析, 但每个异常都和 "qualification 范围之外运行 + OFT1/2 未根因 + 数据采样率不足" 三件事纠缠。 任何单看一个异常 = 漏掉系统性问题。PIT analyzes the 4 hardware anomalies separately, but each is entangled with "operating outside qualification scope + OFT1/2 unexplained anomalies + insufficient data sample rates". Analyzing one alone misses the systemic pattern.

A1 · p25-58

SM RCS Thruster Fail-offSM RCS Thruster Fail-off

ISS 对接期间 5 个 thrusters 触发 fail-off FDIR → 临时失去 6DOF。 In-situ 恢复 4 个 → 完成对接。 最可能直接原因: (i) 两相氧化剂流 (vaporization + cavitation); (ii) Teflon poppet 挤出 → 氧化剂阀阻塞; (iii) GNC 机械指令。During ISS docking 5 thrusters triggered fail-off FDIR → temporary loss of 6DOF. In-situ recovered 4 → completed docking. Most probable direct causes: (i) two-phase oxidizer flow (vaporization + cavitation); (ii) Teflon poppet extrusion in oxidizer valve; (iii) mechanical GNC firing demand.

A2 · p24

CM RCS Jet FailureCM RCS Jet Failure

下降时 1 个 thruster 失火 → 系统降至 零 fault tolerance。 Leading theory: 残存推进剂 + CO₂ → carbazic acid 腐蚀。 直接后果: 失去返回阶段冗余。1 thruster failed to fire during descent → system dropped to zero fault tolerance. Leading theory: residual propellant + CO₂ → carbazic acid corrosion. Direct consequence: loss of return-phase redundancy.

A3 · p71-86

Helium Manifold Leaks (7/8)Helium Manifold Leaks (7/8)

8 个 SM helium manifolds 中 7 个 任务期间泄漏。 最可能直接原因: 密封材料与 NTO (nitrogen tetroxide) 不兼容 → 退化 + 泄漏。 贡献因素: O-ring 尺寸 / gland fill / squeeze 公差。7 of 8 SM helium manifolds leaked during the mission. Most probable direct cause: seal material incompatibility with NTO → degradation + leaks. Contributing: O-ring sizing, gland fill, squeeze tolerances.

A4 · p89-98

Deorbit Fault-Tolerance GapDeorbit Fault-Tolerance Gap

推进系统 缺少 2-fault tolerance for deorbit burns。 这是 设计阶段遗留缺陷, 但一直到 CFT 预发射才被识别。 直接后果: 在轨返回方案的关键冗余缺失。Propulsion system lacked 2-fault tolerance for deorbit burns. This was a design-stage flaw, not identified until CFT pre-launch. Direct consequence: critical redundancy gap in on-orbit return scenario.

"There is no substitute for test. Flight tests and integrated ground tests are ideal for understanding whole system interactions." Flight tests and integrated ground tests are ideal for understanding whole system interactions." (p148) — PIT names "insufficient qualification testing" as root organizational factor OF1, spanning A1 / A3 / A4. A2 (CM RCS corrosion) is a separate reuse-environment issue.

RC1 · p147

合同前硬件采购 → 风险共担失衡Pre-award hardware acquisition → imbalanced shared risk

Boeing 在 NASA 最终合同签订之前就已采购硬件 → 后续任何 NASA 反馈都成 "moot"。 NASA staffing plans post-award 没按 provider 节奏定制, 早期重点偏向 SpaceX。 深层假设: "Boeing 在载人航天上更有经验"。Boeing procured hardware before NASA final contract → any subsequent NASA feedback was "moot". NASA staffing plans post-award weren't tailored per provider's schedule and culture, with early focus on SpaceX maturity. Underlying assumption: "Boeing more experienced in human spaceflight".

R.54 [OP]
RC2 · p148

Boeing 系统工程不足, 硬件超 qualification 范围Boeing inadequate systems engineering, hardware outside qualification

Boeing 没充分应用系统工程的集成过程, 没验证所有操作环境 / 生命周期阶段 / 使用场景。 例: Helium softgoods 暴露 NTO → 漏; SM RCS 热 + 机械需求 → 推力下降; CM RCS 复用环境腐蚀 → jet 失效。 Boeing 自述 "over-emphasis on heritage design"。Boeing didn't adequately apply systems engineering and integration processes to verify all operational environments, lifecycle phases, and use cases. Examples: helium softgoods NTO exposure → leaks; SM RCS heat + mechanical demand → reduced thrust; CM RCS reuse-environment corrosion → jet failure. Boeing self-described "over-emphasis on heritage design".

R.55 [Boeing]
RC3 · p149

CCP "两个 provider 必须成功" 文化 → 风险接受取代深查CCP "two providers must succeed" culture → risk acceptance over deep investigation

CCP 顶层目标 "两个 provider 都要成功" → 技术讨论被风险接受压倒, 抑制 additional investigation。 受访者描述 "contractor going away / pulling out" 作为不挑战 Boeing 的理由。 报告原话: "this root cause is specific to the history of the anomalies seen on CFT, several of those interviewed denoted no change in this culture, post-CFT"。 关键警告: 文化问题到 2025 年中仍未改变。CCP top-level objective "two providers must succeed" → technical discussions dominated by risk acceptance, suppressing additional investigation. Interviewees cited "contractor going away / pulling out" as rationale for not challenging Boeing. Report quote: "this root cause is specific to the history of the anomalies seen on CFT, several of those interviewed denoted no change in this culture, post-CFT". Critical warning: cultural issue unchanged as of mid-2025.

08 · Type A mishap 分类争议08 · Type A mishap classification debateEXECUTIVE SUMMARY

NASA Procedural Requirements 8621.1D 定义 mishap 4 类 (Type A / B / C / D), Type A 最严重 = 损失 ≥ $2M 或 永久失能 / 死亡 / 飞行器损坏。 PIT 在 CFT 是否达 Type A 给出双方观点, 最终推荐追溯分类。NASA Procedural Requirements 8621.1D defines 4 mishap tiers (Type A / B / C / D); Type A is most severe = ≥ $2M loss or permanent disability / death / vehicle destruction. PIT gives both sides on whether CFT met Type A, ultimately recommending retroactive classification.

FOR · Type A

支持追溯分类Arguments for retroactive Type A

"失去对接轴受控飞行" = flight control loss on docking axis = NASA NPR 8621.1D 定义的 Type A criteria。 5 个 thrusters 触发 fail-off FDIR = 不止 "close call"。 从任务延长 (8-14 天 → 93 天) + 最终无载人返回的事实序列看, 属于飞行器安全能力的重大损失。"Loss of controlled flight on docking axis" = flight control loss = NASA NPR 8621.1D Type A criteria. 5 thrusters triggering fail-off FDIR exceeds "close call". The fact sequence of mission extension (8-14 days → 93 days) + eventual uncrewed return represents major loss of vehicle safety capability.

AGAINST · HVCC

反方: High Visibility Close CallCounter: High Visibility Close Call

In-situ 故障排查恢复 4/5 thrusters → 控制恢复 → 安全完成对接 = 没有永久失能 / 没有飞行器永久损失。 严格按 NPR 8621.1D 文字, 这种情况更接近 "high visibility close call" 而非 Type A。 反方论据支持事后追溯为更轻类别。In-situ troubleshooting recovered 4/5 thrusters → control restored → safe docking completed = no permanent disability, no permanent vehicle loss. Strictly by NPR 8621.1D letter, this case is closer to "high visibility close call" than Type A. Counter-argument supports lighter retroactive classification.

PIT 决策

PIT 最终建议PIT's recommendation

PIT 选择追溯 Type A。 论据: 报告 "保留" 而非 "降级" mishap 分类, 触发 NPR 8621.1D 全套调查 + 整改 + NASA 内部监督流程。 "建议追溯" 让 NASA 上层决定是否接受, 但 PIT 自身明确推荐更严类别以驱动整改。PIT chose retroactive Type A. Argument: the report "preserves" rather than "downgrades" the mishap classification, triggering the full NPR 8621.1D investigation + corrective action + NASA internal oversight pipeline. "Recommend retroactive" leaves final acceptance to NASA leadership, but PIT clearly recommends the stricter category to drive corrective action.

"The PIT determined that the loss of 6DOF control during ISS rendezvous meets the criteria for a Type A mishap under NASA Procedural Requirements (NPR 8621.1D) for a loss of controlled flight on the docking axis due to thruster failures.
