01 · OPERATING LOGICS

四种制造“未来”的方式Four ways to manufacture “the future”

古老预测术并非同一种东西。它们有的追踪天体周期，有的引入随机性，有的解释模糊文本，有的依赖个人启示。区分“机制”比争论“信或不信”更有用。Ancient divinations are not one thing. Some track celestial cycles, some inject randomness, some interpret elastic texts, and some rely on personal revelation. Distinguishing mechanisms is more useful than arguing over belief.

01 / CYCLE

周期映射Cycle mapping

把天体、历法或季节的重复运动映射到人格、事件与时机。代表：西方占星、吠陀占星。Map repeating celestial or calendrical motion onto character, events and timing. Seen in Western astrology and Jyotiṣa.

02 / CHANCE

随机触发Random prompt

用蓍草、铜钱、牌面或裂纹打断惯性思维，再由传统符号库赋义。代表：《易经》、塔罗、甲骨。Use stalks, coins, cards or cracks to interrupt habitual thought, then interpret through a symbolic canon. I Ching, tarot, oracle bones.

03 / TEXT

寓言索引Allegorical indexing

把历史事件重新对应到含混图像或诗句。代表：《推背图》、诺查丹玛斯式预言。Retrofit events to ambiguous images or verse. Seen in the Tui Bei Tu and Nostradamus-style prophecy.

04 / REVELATION

启示中介Revelatory mediation

由灵媒、梦境或自称未来人提供信息；权威来自身份与叙事，而非可重复程序。A medium, dreamer or claimed time traveller supplies information; authority comes from identity and narrative, not repeatable procedure.

02 · SYSTEM ATLAS

九种预测体系，逐层拆解Nine systems, decoded layer by layer

每张卡区分“历史上确有其事”与“它能否预测未来”。证据标记评价的是字面预测力；文化、心理或叙事价值另行说明。Each card separates “this practice historically existed” from “it predicts the future.” Evidence badges rate literal forecasting power; cultural and reflective value is treated separately.

西方占星Western astrology

HELLENISTIC MEDITERRANEAN · c. 2ND C. CE
运作方式Mechanism

用出生时间、地点计算行星、星座、宫位与相位；再以行运、推运或年度技术推断阶段性主题。Birth time and place generate planets, signs, houses and aspects; transits, directions or annual techniques are read as life themes.

现代用法Modern use

更适合作为自我叙事、关系对话与复盘节律；不应单独决定招聘、投资、医疗或重大风险。Best treated as self-narrative, relationship dialogue or a review cadence—not a sole basis for hiring, investing, medicine or material risk.

主要误差Failure mode

巴纳姆效应、确认偏误、事后挑选；1985 年《Nature》双盲试验未发现高于机会水平的匹配能力。Barnum statements, confirmation bias and selective hindsight; a 1985 Nature double-blind test did not support above-chance matching.

[01] PTOLEMY · [02] NATURE
天体周期Celestial cycleLITERAL · D

吠陀占星 · JyotiṣaVedic astrology · Jyotiṣa

SOUTH ASIA · LAYERED TRADITIONS
运作方式Mechanism

现代命理常用恒星黄道、月宿、宫位、行星周期（daśā）、行运与择时（muhūrta）。早期《吠陀支天文》核心其实是祭祀历法。Modern practice often uses a sidereal zodiac, lunar mansions, houses, planetary periods, transits and electional timing. Early Vedāṅga Jyotiṣa was primarily calendrical ritual timing.

现代用法Modern use

用于人生阶段叙事、婚配、命名、仪式和决策时机。可借用“多周期并行”做长期复盘，但不要把文化传统误当因果证据。Used for life-stage narratives, matching, naming, ritual and timing. Its nested cycles can inspire long-range reviews, without treating tradition as causal evidence.

历史提醒Historical caution

“吠陀天文历法”与后世“出生星盘命理”不是同一层；南亚传统也吸收并改造了希腊与两河星术。Vedic calendrics and later natal horoscopy are distinct layers; South Asian astral sciences also transformed Greek and Mesopotamian inputs.

[03] VEDIC HERITAGE · [04] BEINORIUS
符号随机Symbolic chanceREFLECTIVE · C

《易经》I Ching · Yijing

CHINA · 1ST MILLENNIUM BCE TEXTUAL LAYERS
运作方式Mechanism

以蓍草或铜钱产生六爻卦象；本卦、动爻与变卦连接到判断、爻辞及历代注疏。Yarrow stalks or coins generate six lines; the primary hexagram, changing lines and resulting hexagram connect to judgments and commentaries.

现代用法Modern use

把随机性当“观点扰动器”：强迫团队从相反角色、时势与变化路径重述问题；把解释写入决策日志。Use chance as a perspective perturbation: make a team restate the issue through opposite roles, timing and transformation, then log the interpretation.

证据边界Evidence boundary

古本与占筮传统有坚实文献史；“随机所得卦象能超自然预见事件”尚无可靠实证。心理价值来自反思，不等于预测准确。The textual and divinatory history is well documented; supernatural event prediction is not. Reflective usefulness is not forecast accuracy.

[05] COLUMBIA · [06] ESSEX
符号随机Symbolic chancePROCESS · C

甲骨卜辞Oracle-bone divination

SHANG CHINA · c. 13TH–11TH C. BCE
运作方式Mechanism

王室就天气、战争、收成、疾病等提出命题，加热龟甲或兽骨，根据裂纹占断；部分甲骨还记录验辞与结果。Royal courts posed questions on weather, war, harvest and illness, heated shell or bone, read the cracks, and sometimes recorded verification or outcome.

现代用法Modern use

最值得继承的不是裂纹，而是“问题—判断—日期—结果”链条：它近似早期预测登记册。The transferable insight is not crack-reading but the question–judgment–date–outcome chain: an ancestor of a forecast registry.

证据边界Evidence boundary

甲骨能证明制度、文字与国家决策实践，却不能证明祖先或裂纹带来超自然信息。Oracle bones document institutions, writing and state decisions, not supernatural information from ancestors or cracks.

[07] PENN MUSEUM
预言文本Prophetic textLITERAL · D

《推背图》Tui Bei Tu

CHINA · ATTRIBUTED TO TANG, MULTIPLE LATER VERSIONS
运作方式Mechanism

常见本以六十幅图、谶语与颂诗串联王朝兴替；读者把人名、方位、字形与当代事件重新对应。Popular editions pair sixty images with cryptic verse across dynastic change; readers remap names, directions and characters to current events.

现代用法Modern use

可分析时代焦虑与政治想象，或用作“极端叙事”压力测试；不能把版本不稳定的文本当时间表。Useful for studying public anxiety and political imagination, or as an extreme-narrative stress test—not as a timetable.

主要误差Failure mode

版本差异、后出增改、任意换序和事后匹配。学术研究指出，今日常见的“金批本”在 1915 年出版市场上突然出现。Variant editions, later interpolation, reordered chapters and postdiction. Scholarship notes the popular annotated edition appeared abruptly in the 1915 market.

[08] CAMBRIDGE JRAS
符号随机Symbolic chanceREFLECTIVE · C

塔罗Tarot

RENAISSANCE ITALY → 18TH-C. FRENCH DIVINATION
运作方式Mechanism

洗牌、抽牌、放入牌阵，再把图像、位置、正逆与提问串成故事。Shuffle, draw and place cards in a spread, then join imagery, position, orientation and question into a narrative.

现代用法Modern use

作为投射式自我反思、教练提问或创意写作提示；最好由提问者自己说明“这张图让我想起什么”。As projective self-reflection, coaching questions or creative prompts; the querent should explain what the image evokes for them.

历史提醒Historical caution

塔罗最初是文艺复兴时期的纸牌游戏，十八世纪末才与占卜关联；“源自古埃及密教”缺乏历史证据。Tarot began as a Renaissance card game and became linked to fortune-telling only in the late eighteenth century; ancient-Egypt origins are unsupported.

[09] OXFORD · [10] V&A
预言文本Prophetic textLITERAL · D

预言诗与诺查丹玛斯Prophetic verse & Nostradamus

EUROPE · EARLY MODERN PRINT CULTURE
运作方式Mechanism

以四行诗、隐喻、古地名、占星意象与语言变体制造高解释空间；媒体在事件后寻找对应句。Quatrains, metaphor, archaic place names, astral imagery and language shifts create wide interpretive latitude; events are matched afterward.

现代用法Modern use

把它当“叙事弹性”示范：团队先写清什么结果会证明预测错误，再阅读含混文本，体验事后解释的诱惑。Use it to demonstrate narrative elasticity: define disconfirmation first, then read the ambiguous text and notice the pull of hindsight.

主要误差Failure mode

翻译自由度、关键词挑选、事件基数极大、忽略未命中。越模糊的句子，越容易在事后“准确”。Translation freedom, keyword selection, huge event base rates and ignored misses. The more elastic the line, the easier the post-hoc “hit.”

[11] FORER EFFECT
灵媒启示MediumshipREPEATABILITY · D

英国灵媒与心灵研究British mediumship & psychical research

BRITAIN · 19TH-C. SPIRITUALISM TO PRESENT
运作方式Mechanism

恍惚发言、自动书写、千里眼、亡者通信或直觉图像；流程高度依赖个人状态与提问者反馈。Trance speech, automatic writing, clairvoyance, spirit communication or intuitive imagery; the process depends heavily on practitioner and sitter feedback.

现代用法Modern use

伦敦心灵研究学会自 1882 年起尝试收集、核查与实验。组织层面可借鉴的是“匿名、盲法、独立记录”，不是启示本身。The London-based SPR has collected, investigated and experimented since 1882. Organizations can borrow blinding, anonymity and independent records—not the revelation claim.

证据边界Evidence boundary

历史档案丰富，争议也持续；尚无稳定、可重复、足以指导高风险决策的预知方法。The archive is rich and controversy persists; no stable, repeatable precognitive method is established for high-stakes decisions.

[12] SPR HISTORY

“未来人”约翰·提托“Time traveller” John Titor

INTERNET FORUMS · 2000–2001
运作方式Mechanism

账号 TimeTravel_0 自称来自 2036 年，发布机器照片、IBM 5100 细节与战争预测；失败后可用“平行世界线”解释。TimeTravel_0 claimed to come from 2036, posting machine photos, IBM 5100 details and war forecasts; misses could be excused by divergent timelines.

现代用法Modern use

这是网络时代的完美媒体素养样本：保留原帖、列出全部可证伪断言、设截止日，命中与落空同权计数。A model media-literacy case: preserve original posts, list every falsifiable claim, set deadlines, and count hits and misses symmetrically.

结果Outcome

美国 2008 内战、2015 核冲突等核心预言均未发生；身份从未证实。技术小知识并不能验证整套叙事。Core claims such as U.S. civil war by 2008 and nuclear conflict by 2015 failed; identity remains unverified. A technical curiosity does not validate the whole story.

04 · ANCIENT → MODERN

保留问题，
升级方法。Keep the question.
Upgrade the method.

古人真正留给我们的，是对不确定性的仪式化处理：暂停、提问、看见不同路径、记录判断。现代未来学把这些动作变成可审计流程。The durable inheritance is a ritual for uncertainty: pause, ask, see alternate paths, record judgment. Modern futures practice turns those moves into auditable process.

像甲骨一样，把问题刻下来Inscribe the question like an oracle bone

把“AI 会改变公司吗？”改为“截至 2027-06-30，至少 30% 的客服会话是否由 AI 在无人接管下闭环？”Turn “Will AI change us?” into “By 2027-06-30, will AI close at least 30% of support conversations without handoff?”

RESOLVABLE · DEADLINE · THRESHOLD

像《易经》一样，主动扰动视角Perturb perspective like the I Ching

强制生成“增长、停滞、反转、意外旁路”四种解释。随机提示可用，但结论必须回到证据。Force four frames: growth, stagnation, reversal and an unexpected side path. Random prompts are allowed; conclusions return to evidence.

REFRAME · COUNTERFACTUAL · EVIDENCE

像星历一样，建立更新节律Build an update cadence like an ephemeris

设每月或每季度更新，而不是只在发生大事时回看。每次只根据新增证据调整概率，并解释为什么。Update monthly or quarterly, not only after drama. Change probability only for new evidence, and state why.

CADENCE · BAYESIAN UPDATE · CHANGELOG

SIGNALS · SCENARIOS · STRESS TEST

让群体智慧接受计分Make collective judgment keep score

06 · THE SIMULATION QUESTION

我们真的活在虚拟世界吗？Are we really living in a simulation?

诚实答案：目前不知道，也没有决定性证据。 占卜“偶尔说中”、量子力学反直觉、宇宙可被数学描述，都不是模拟世界的直接证据。The honest answer: we do not know, and there is no decisive evidence. Occasional divination “hits,” quantum strangeness and mathematical laws are not direct evidence of a simulated world.

模拟假说目前更像高价值的哲学思想实验，而不是被验证的物理理论。The simulation hypothesis is currently a valuable philosophical thought experiment, not a verified physical theory.
1

文明未达到“后人类”阶段Civilizations rarely reach a posthuman stage

技术文明在能运行祖先模拟前就停滞或灭绝。Technological civilizations stall or end before ancestor simulations.

2

先进文明几乎不运行祖先模拟Advanced civilizations rarely run ancestor simulations

出于伦理、兴趣、资源或治理原因，它们选择不这样做。Ethics, interest, resources or governance prevent it.

3

模拟观察者数量压倒真实观察者Simulated observers vastly outnumber originals

若前两项都不成立，我们就可能处在模拟中——这是条件论证，不是证明。If the first two fail, we may be simulated—a conditional argument, not proof.

PHILOSOPHY · OPEN

博斯特罗姆论证Bostrom's argument

2003 年论文提出三难，不是“99% 证明我们在游戏里”。它依赖意识可在不同基质实现、文明存续、模拟动机与观察者抽样等假设。The 2003 paper gives a trilemma, not “99% proof.” It depends on substrate-independent minds, civilizational survival, simulation motives and observer sampling.

PHYSICS · CONDITIONAL

可检验的窄版本Narrow testable versions

Beane 等人研究了“宇宙是立方时空格点数值模拟”这一特定假设，推导高能宇宙射线可能信号；即使未发现，也只排除某类格点模拟。Beane and colleagues examined one specific cubic lattice simulation and possible cosmic-ray signatures. A null result would exclude only that narrow architecture.

PRACTICE · UNCHANGED

就算是真的，又怎样？Even if true—then what?

痛苦、责任与后果仍在我们的经验层真实存在。最稳健的策略不变：保留可逆性、更新信念、保护他人，并对所有“神谕”要求可证伪。Pain, responsibility and consequences remain real at our experiential level. Robust strategy is unchanged: preserve reversibility, update beliefs, protect others and demand falsifiability from every oracle.
