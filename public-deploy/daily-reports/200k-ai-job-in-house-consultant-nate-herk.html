8. 原汁原味 Quotes (EN verbatim + 中文翻译)

  1. "the people who know how to use AI get ahead of the people who don't"

→ 懂 AI 的人永远领先不懂的人。

  1. "the value is in judgment, taste, solving ambiguity, deciding what problems to point AI at and what problems to not point AI at"

→ 人类的价值在判断力、品味、解决歧义、决定哪些问题该让 AI 解、哪些不该。

  1. "Sam Altman said that the idea guys are about to have their day in the sun"

→ Sam Altman 说,创意人终于要等到自己的黄金期了。

  1. "the builder, that's kind of more of the pharmacist, and the in-house AI consultant, that's the doctor. And the doctor is the one who gets paid the real money."

→ Builder 更像药剂师(给什么拿什么),内部 AI 顾问才是医生(决定要拿什么)。医生才是拿高薪的那个。

  1. "if the AI gets it a little wrong, nobody gets hurt"

→ AI 出错没致命后果 — 这是 4 步路线图第 1 步要挑的 "low-risk task"。

  1. "automating annoyances doesn't really grow a business. What does is attacking the constraints"

→ 自动化烦人事不增长业务,增长业务的是攻破瓶颈。

  1. "we doubled our customers tomorrow, what process or what thing would break first?"

→ 客户翻倍的话,哪个流程会先崩?这就是你的第一个瓶颈项目。

  1. "across these five automations, I'm giving the team back the equivalent of a full-time hire every single year"

→ 5 个自动化 = 每年还给团队 1 个全职人力 — 用这个数字谈预算。

  1. "You're not asking them for a favor. You're proposing the actual role and the title."

→ 你不是求老板给你份工作,而是提议一个新职位 + 头衔。

  1. "the people stepping into these roles are the ones who saw it coming early"

→ 拿到这些职位的人,都是早期看到趋势的。

10. 一句话总结 · TL;DR

AI 时代最稀缺的职业不是 builder,是 doctor。 Builder 会随 AI 工具民主化被 commoditize,但判断力、判断什么该 AI 化、什么不该,这些永远稀缺。"内部 AI 顾问" 角色正在从无到 76% 普及,先上车的人吃肉。
