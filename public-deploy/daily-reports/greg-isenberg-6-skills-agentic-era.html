核心论点

核心预览 (1 blockquote + 5 行): Greg Isenberg (LCA 创始人, 662K 订阅) 在 29:53 单人 episode 里主张: 当 AI 可以 build 几乎任何东西、写几乎任何东西的时候, 有 6 个 skill 反而越涨价值, 不是越跌. 他给每个 skill 一个具体的 "first rep" (本周可以开始的练习) — 这是视频最有价值的部分. 框架: 1) Agent + Local Models, 2) Marketer who builds distribution, 3) Robotics engineer who builds + sources hardware, 4) Curator who yaps + makes short-form, 5) Builder Distributor (一人公司), 6) IRL community builder. 最锐利的一句: "Pick one and get dangerous. Pick two and you have leverage. Pick three and you become the kind of person that everyone wants on the team, in the room, or building the company." — 15:23 收尾 thesis.

关键 takeaway (6 lines)

  • "Pick 1 + get dangerous, pick 2 + leverage, pick 3 + irreplaceable" — Greg 收尾 thesis. Skill stacking > 单点深度.
  • First reps > frameworks — 视频的金矿是具体的 48 小时 / 7 天 / 周末练习. 立即可用.
  • Local AI ≠ 小模型 — Greg: "Even if local models go smaller, you learn the architecture for the future. You learn which jobs need a giant brain and which jobs just need a reliable worker that never sleeps." (02:54)
  • Distribution beats product — 当任何人都能 ship SaaS / landing page, 瓶颈变成 "能不能让人在乎".
  • Robotics = atoms, 不只是 pixels — SO-100 + LeRobot + Alibaba sourcing 是新的低成本栈. 软件人桥接到硬件有 edge.
  • IRL ≠ 怀旧 — 稀缺流向 trust/context/belonging. Recap email 是 unlock — 房间 → 网络.
