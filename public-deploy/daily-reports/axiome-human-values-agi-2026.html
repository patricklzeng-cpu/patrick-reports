01 / THE LONG ARCHIVE价值系统的长历史A LONG HISTORY OF VALUE
12个切面，不是一条进步线CUTS, NOT A LADDER OF PROGRESS

哲学史不是希腊一路走到硅谷。不同文明反复回答相似问题，却把善锚定在宇宙秩序、德性、解脱、关系、尊严、自由或万物共生之中。成文档案带有书写、帝国与殖民保存偏差。Philosophy is not a road from Greece to Silicon Valley. Traditions revisit similar questions while anchoring the good in order, virtue, liberation, relation, dignity, freedom, or more-than-human life. Written archives carry literacy, imperial, and colonial survival bias.

LENS / 透镜
01c. 2600–1200 BCE
Nile · Mesopotamia · Vedic India

宇宙即秩序COSMOS AS ORDER

价值首先不是个人选择，而是维系天地、城邦与祖先秩序的义务：玛阿特、神法、Ṛta。Value begins less as personal choice than as the duty to sustain cosmic, civic, and ancestral order: ma’at, divine law, ṛta.

EDITOR’S NOTE这些传统并非同一种哲学；并置只显示一个反复出现的答案：善，意味着让世界不失序。These are not one philosophy. Their juxtaposition reveals a recurring answer: the good keeps a world from falling out of order.

S01
02c. 800–200 BCE
China · India · Greece

平行的伦理革命PARALLEL ETHICAL REVOLUTIONS

仁与礼、道与无为、德性与幸福、业与解脱、非暴力与非绝对论：好生活成为可修炼、可论证的问题。Ren and ritual, dao and non-coercion, virtue and flourishing, karma and liberation, non-violence and non-absolutism turn the good life into a practice and an argument.

EDITOR’S NOTE“轴心时代”是有争议的框架；这里不主张单一起源或同步进步。The “Axial Age” is a contested frame; no single origin or synchronized progress is claimed here.

S02S03S04S05
03c. 300 BCE–500 CE
Mediterranean · South & Central Asia

道德圈越过城邦THE MORAL CIRCLE WIDENS

斯多葛世界公民、佛教慈悲、耆那教不害与早期普世宗教，把道德关切推向陌生人乃至众生。Stoic cosmopolitanism, Buddhist compassion, Jain non-violence, and universal religions extend concern beyond the city and kin—sometimes toward all beings.

EDITOR’S NOTE普遍主义扩大关切，也可能压平差异；包容与同化始终相邻。Universalism can widen concern and flatten difference; inclusion and assimilation remain uneasy neighbors.

S03S04S05
04c. 600–1300
Islamic world · Jewish & Christian thought

启示与理性的谈判REVELATION NEGOTIATES WITH REASON

神命、理性、德性、自然法与公共福祉被置于同一张论证桌上；善是否独立于神，成为核心难题。Divine command, reason, virtue, natural law, and public welfare share one argumentative table. Whether the good is independent of God becomes a central problem.

EDITOR’S NOTE中世纪不是理性的空白期，而是多语种知识网络的高密度时期。The medieval world was not reason’s blank interval but a dense, multilingual knowledge network.

S06
05longue durée · c. 1000–1700 texts
East Asia · African oral & textual traditions

人格生于关系PERSONHOOD THROUGH RELATION

宋明儒学的修身与天理、非洲共同体伦理的品格与道德人格，都拒绝把“自我”看作孤立原子。Neo-Confucian cultivation and African accounts of character and moral personhood refuse to treat the self as an isolated atom.

EDITOR’S NOTE这里的年代只标示部分成文脉络，不是口述传统的起点。“非洲伦理”内部极其多样；Ubuntu 也不是整片大陆的单一代名词。The date marks some textual lineages, not the origin of oral traditions. African ethics is internally diverse; Ubuntu is not a label for an entire continent.

S02S07S08
06c. 1500–1800
Europe · Atlantic world

自主理性的诞生THE INVENTION OF AUTONOMOUS REASON

自然权利、社会契约、功利、公共理性与自我立法，让个人从既定秩序中获得规范权威。Natural rights, social contract, utility, public reason, and self-legislation give the individual normative authority against inherited order.

EDITOR’S NOTE启蒙的普遍人同时与殖民、奴役和性别排斥共存；价值宣言从未自动兑现。The Enlightenment’s universal person coexisted with empire, slavery, and gender exclusion. Declared values never implement themselves.

S09
071770s–1948
Transnational struggles

尊严成为普遍主张DIGNITY BECOMES A UNIVERSAL CLAIM

废奴、工人、妇女、反殖民与民族解放运动，迫使“谁算作完整的人”不断扩容；1948 年《世界人权宣言》将其制度化。Abolitionist, labor, women’s, anti-colonial, and liberation movements force the category of the full human outward; the 1948 UDHR gives it institutional form.

EDITOR’S NOTE这不是西方赠予世界的礼物，而是跨文化代表、斗争与二战灾难后的不完整共识。Not a gift from “the West” to the world, but an incomplete consensus shaped by cross-cultural authorship, struggle, and catastrophe.

S10
08c. 1800–1960
Industrial modernity

意义失去保证MEANING LOSES ITS GUARANTEE

马克思追问物质权力，尼采重估价值，存在主义把自由与责任放入无保证的世界；实用主义则以经验后果检验观念。Marx interrogates material power, Nietzsche revalues values, existentialism locates freedom in a world without guarantees, and pragmatism tests ideas by consequences.

EDITOR’S NOTE现代主体获得自由，也背负了制造意义的重量。The modern subject gains freedom—and the burden of making meaning.

S11
09c. 1900–2000
Feminist · Africana · postcolonial

“普遍”接受审问THE UNIVERSAL STANDS TRIAL

关怀伦理、女性主义、非洲侨民与后殖民思想指出：抽象中立常隐藏身体、照护、种族、帝国与发言位置。Care ethics, feminist, Africana, and postcolonial thought expose how abstract neutrality can conceal bodies, care, race, empire, and standpoint.

EDITOR’S NOTE批判不是放弃共同价值，而是追问：谁定义、谁付出、谁能反对。Critique need not abandon shared values; it asks who defines them, who pays, and who may contest them.

S08S12
10longue durée · academic turn c. 1960–now
Planetary & Indigenous turns

道德圈越过人类BEYOND THE HUMAN CIRCLE

动物伦理、环境伦理、生态女性主义与原住民关系知识，挑战人类中心主义，把生态系统、后代与非人生命带入价值计算。Animal and environmental ethics, ecofeminism, and Indigenous relational knowledge challenge anthropocentrism by bringing ecosystems, future generations, and non-human life into view.

EDITOR’S NOTE原住民知识远早于现代学院命名；此处年代仅指全球学术与制度的可见转向。“自然有价值”也不自动解决利益冲突。Indigenous knowledge long predates its academic visibility; this date marks a global institutional turn, not an origin. Ecological value also leaves hard conflicts unresolved.

S13S14
11c. 1990–now
Networked societies

注意力成为战场ATTENTION BECOMES TERRITORY

数据、推荐系统与平台权力让隐私、认知自由、可解释性和信息公共空间成为新的基本价值。Data, recommender systems, and platform power turn privacy, cognitive liberty, explainability, and the information commons into basic values.

EDITOR’S NOTE数字伦理并非虚拟层；它重新分配现实中的看见、机会与权力。Digital ethics is not a virtual layer. It redistributes visibility, opportunity, and power in the physical world.

当模型能生成、劝服、规划并代为行动，模糊的价值宣言必须变成目标、权限、红线、申诉机制与资源预算。As models generate, persuade, plan, and act, vague declarations must become objectives, permissions, red lines, appeals, and resource budgets.

03 / THE MORAL STRESS TEST

AGI 最大的哲学冲击，不是“机器有没有价值”。AGI’s deepest shock is not whether machines “have values.”

而是人类第一次必须把含混、冲突、常靠例外维持的价值，编译成系统可执行的目标、权限与拒绝权。It is that humans must compile ambiguous, conflicting, exception-ridden values into objectives, permissions, and rights of refusal.

WHY IT BREAKS / 断裂机制

若系统在推理、创造与规划上超过多数人，“人类更聪明”就无法继续支撑人类特殊地位。我们要么诉诸物种，要么把尊严锚定于脆弱、感受、关系或不可替代的生命史。

If systems outperform most people in reasoning, creation, and planning, superior intelligence can no longer ground human specialness. Dignity must anchor in vulnerability, sentience, relation, or an irreplaceable lived history—not a leaderboard.

POSSIBLE GAIN / 可能收益

反而能强化残障者、儿童与非人生命不因能力较低而贬值的原则。It can strengthen the principle that disability, childhood, or lower capability never reduce worth.

FAILURE MODE / 失败模式

以“超级理性”为名建立机器技术官僚制。Machine technocracy justified by “superior reason.”

WATCH FOR / 观察信号

法律和机构开始明确区分能力、法律权利与道德地位。Institutions explicitly separate capability, legal rights, and moral status.

01FORECAST ≠ FACT

AGI 也许不会以单一事件“到来”AGI may never arrive as one clean event

基准进步不等于稳健的通用能动性。用确定语气讨论时间表，会把哲学变成营销。Benchmark gains are not robust general agency. Treating timelines as certain turns philosophy into marketing.

02NO FLUENCY TEST

能力、意识、权利是三条轴Capability, consciousness, and rights are three axes

会说“我痛”不是痛的证据；不会说话也不是没有痛的证据。流畅度不能代替意识科学。Saying “I hurt” is not proof of pain; silence is not proof of its absence. Fluency cannot replace consciousness science.

03FOLLOW THE POWER

技术不会独自决定价值Technology does not determine values alone

同一模型可支持公共教育，也可集中监控。所有权、法律、商业模式和组织权力往往比架构更关键。The same model can widen education or centralize surveillance. Ownership, law, business models, and institutions may matter more than architecture.

04NO INEVITABILITY

历史不是道德升级直线History is not a moral upgrade path

权利扩张常与新型排斥并存。时间轴展示问题如何迁移，不宣称文明越来越好。Rights expansion often coexists with new exclusion. The timeline tracks migrating questions, not inevitable improvement.

05SCARCITY MOVES

后工作不等于后稀缺Post-work is not post-scarcity

土地、照护、能源、注意力、地位和政治权力仍然稀缺；AGI 甚至可能加速其中一些竞争。Land, care, energy, attention, status, and political power remain scarce; AGI may intensify some of them.

06WHO CAN REFUSE?

“全球价值”可能是地方权力的扩音器“Global values” may amplify local power

一套统一对齐可能把多数文化、原住民知识和弱势群体压成训练数据。多元必须包含实质否决权。A universal alignment may flatten cultures, Indigenous knowledge, and marginalized groups into training data. Pluralism needs real veto power.

06 / PRE-AGI CHARTER不等 AGI 的七条原则SEVEN PRINCIPLES THAT DO NOT WAIT FOR AGI

未来价值不会被发现。它会被制度化。Future values will not be discovered. They will be institutionalized.

LIMITS / 边界

这不是哲学史教科书，也不可能代表每个传统内部的分歧。“人类价值体系”没有统一版本；任何压缩都会损失语境。AGI 仍是定义与时间表均有争议的假设。本项目的目标，是提供一套可检验的提问结构。

This is not a philosophy textbook and cannot represent internal disputes within every tradition. There is no single “human value system”; compression always loses context. AGI remains contested in definition and timeline. The aim is a testable architecture of questions.
