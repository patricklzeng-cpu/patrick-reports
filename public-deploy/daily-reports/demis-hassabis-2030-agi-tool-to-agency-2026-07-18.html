对产品设计的关键启示Implications for product design

一个能调用工具、持续执行任务、拥有规划循环的 agent，仍然不等于它有主观体验。把“会行动”与“有意识”混成一个指标，会让产品规格、安全讨论与哲学讨论全部失焦。An agent that can call tools, sustain task loops, and run planning cycles is still not equivalent to a system with subjective experience. Demis 把能量、物质与信息放在一种“可互换 / 可相互解释”的直觉框架里，并认为从信息处理角度理解生命、AI 与宇宙可能更有穿透力。他还把 AI 视作组织、理解与构造信息对象的系统。Demis places energy, matter, and information into an intuition where they are convertible / mutually interpretable, and reads life, AI, and the universe as information processing. 意识、substrate 与不可闭合的问题Consciousness, Substrate, and the Unclosable Gap

Demis 给出的必要条件候选包括：self-awareness、自我与他者的区分、随时间的连续性。但他同时承认这些条件“可能必要但不充分”。Demis lists candidate necessary conditions: self-awareness, the distinction between self and other, continuity over time. He admits these are likely necessary but not sufficient.

行为相似Behavioral similarity

AI 系统可能越来越表现得像有意识的主体。AI systems may increasingly behave like conscious subjects.

Substrate 差异Substrate divergence

人类之间共享生物 substrate，人与 AI 之间不会。Humans share biological substrate with each other; we will not share it with AI.

Parsimony 失效Parsimony breaks

我们对彼此“同体验”的默认推断无法直接套到 AI 上。Our default inference of shared experience between humans does not transfer to AI.

体验问题The experiential question

体验层面（qualia）目前无法用行为测试完全闭合。The experiential layer (qualia) is not closable by behavior alone.

对人类而言，我们既观察彼此的行为，也共享相同的生物 substrate，因此会默认他人拥有类似体验。人工系统不具备这种 substrate equivalence；即使它表现得像一个有意识的主体，体验问题仍然无法只靠行为测试完全解决。For humans, we both observe each other's behavior and share the same biological substrate, so we default to assuming similar experience. Artificial systems lack that substrate equivalence; even if they behave like conscious subjects, the experiential question cannot be fully closed by behavior alone.
