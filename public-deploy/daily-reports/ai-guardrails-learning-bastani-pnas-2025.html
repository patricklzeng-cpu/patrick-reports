援手，还是拐杖？ A helping hand—or a crutch?

AI 能把眼前的练习成绩推高，也可能让学生在 AI 被撤走后独立作答更差。决定结果的，不只是模型能力，而是界面是否迫使学习发生。 AI can lift performance while assistance is present yet leave students worse off once it disappears. The interface is not neutral: it can either preserve effort or automate it away.

01 / ARGUMENT

论文不是在问“AI 好不好”The paper is not asking whether AI is “good”

它把“有 AI 时做得更好”与“没有 AI 后真的学会”拆成两个结果，再用受控设计比较两种截然不同的 AI 交互方式。It separates assisted performance from independent learning, then tests whether two interface designs produce different transfer outcomes.

02 / EXPERIMENT

实验的关键，是“撤掉工具”The decisive move is taking the tool away

四次 90 分钟课堂把教学、带资源练习、无资源考试串在同一节课里。只有中间练习阶段因随机分组而不同。Four 90-minute sessions place instruction, resource-assisted practice, and an unassisted exam back-to-back. Only the practice environment varies by assignment.

PART 1教师复习 / Teacher review

讲解已学主题与例题；三组完全一致。Previously taught topic + worked examples; identical across arms.

PART 2 · RANDOMIZED辅助练习 / Assisted practice

学生可用课本与笔记；按班级随机获得无 AI、GPT Base 或 GPT Tutor。交卷后，教师向所有组简短讲解正确答案。Textbook and notes for all; AI access depends on assigned arm. After submission, every arm receives a brief answer review.

闭卷、关电脑、无资源；题目与练习概念上高度对应。Closed book, closed laptop; near-transfer problems match practice concepts.

03 / GUARDRAILS

“护栏”不是一句温柔的提示词A guardrail is more than a polite prompt

GPT Tutor 同时改变回答策略与知识底座：它限制直接给答案，还获得教师为每道题准备的正确解、解题步骤、常见误区和提示。GPT Tutor changes both response policy and grounding: it withholds full answers while receiving teacher-authored solutions, steps, common errors, and hints for each problem.

05 / MECHANISM

是 AI 教错了，还是学生没在学？Did AI teach the wrong thing—or replace the learning?

作者比较两个候选机制：错误答案是否误导了学生，或学生是否把 GPT Base 当成复制答案的“拐杖”。证据更支持后者，但机制分析不是随机化中介实验。The authors pit error transmission against cognitive offloading. Evidence favors the “crutch” account, though the mechanism itself is not randomized.

LESS SUPPORTED

H1 · 错误被带进考试 / Error spillover

如果学生认真读了错误解法并内化，练习题上错误率更高的题，应该在对应考试题上留下负面痕迹。If students internalized wrong reasoning, error-prone practice problems should damage their matched exam problems.

  • 错误率显著伤害当下练习成绩。 / Errors hurt practice scores.
  • 但错误率 × GPT Base 在对应考试题上的交互不显著。 / No significant matched-exam spillover.
BETTER SUPPORTED

H2 · 复制替代了思考 / Crutch use

GPT Base 学生更少互动，最常直接索要答案；算术错误与逻辑错误对练习同样破坏，暗示学生甚至没有检查容易发现的错误。Base users interact less and often request answers; arithmetic and logic errors harm practice similarly, consistent with copying rather than checking.

  • Base 首轮第一场的浅层首消息达 67%，Tutor 为 37%。
  • Tutor 学生平均多花 2.046 分钟，即 +13%。

07 / EVIDENCE AUDIT

哪些结论很硬，哪些还只是线索What is established—and what remains a clue

最强的是短期课堂部署对随后的无辅助近迁移考试的因果效应；越往长期、跨学科、具体心理机制外推，证据就越弱。The strongest claim is causal and local: the deployment changed an immediate unassisted near-transfer exam. Long-run, cross-domain, and psychological extrapolations are weaker.

强：短期处理效应 / Strong

无护栏 Base 降低随后的无辅助考试成绩；Tutor 与对照无显著差异。课堂层级随机、预注册、盲评、ITT。Base harms the next unassisted exam; Tutor is statistically neutral. Class assignment, preregistration, blind grading, ITT.

中：拐杖机制 / Suggestive

错误溢出不显著、浅层消息更多、互动更少，共同指向复制答案；但中介行为并未随机化。Behavior and error patterns align with copying, but the mediator is observational.

弱：广泛与长期外推 / Unresolved

不能据此断言所有 AI、所有学科、长期记忆或现代模型都会产生相同结果。This does not establish effects for all AI systems, subjects, long-term retention, or newer models.

学习的定义很近、很短 / Near and short
考试紧接练习，且题目与练习概念上高度相似。这是短期近迁移，不是延迟记忆、远迁移或长期技能。The exam immediately follows practice and uses conceptually similar problems: short-run near transfer, not delayed retention or far transfer.
外部效度有限 / One context
单所土耳其高中、数学、9–11 年级、2023 秋季、GPT-4-0613。模型能力与用户习惯都已变化。One Turkish high school, mathematics, grades 9–11, Fall 2023, GPT-4-0613. Models and user familiarity have since changed.
处理是一个“部署包” / Treatment bundle
对照组无设备，而 GPT 组有笔记本、介绍视频和工具。Control 对 GPT 的比较识别的是完整部署包；Base 对 Tutor 才更直接回答界面设计差异。Control has no device while GPT arms receive laptops, onboarding video, and the tool. Control comparisons estimate a deployment bundle; Base–Tutor more directly isolates design.
护栏成分没有拆开 / Bundled guardrails
Tutor 同时拒绝完整答案、要求展示过程、逐步提示，并注入教师答案与错误库。论文无法判断哪一项贡献最大。Tutor combines work-first interaction, no full solutions, progressive hints, and teacher grounding. Component contributions are not separately identified.
预注册有证据层级 / Preregistration tiers
无辅助考试是预注册主要结果；感知、异质性与对话定性分析属于预注册次要分析。带辅助练习、题目层回归、成绩分散与 GPT 错误率分析为探索性。The unassisted exam is the preregistered primary outcome. Perception, heterogeneity, and qualitative dialogue analysis are secondary; assisted practice, problem-level, dispersion, and error-rate analyses are exploratory.
五个班级场次未按处理执行 / Noncompliance
因电脑迟到、教师误解或界面技术故障，有五个场次转成对照。主分析保留 ITT；剔除后结果几乎相同。Five class-sessions switched to control for logistical reasons. ITT preserves randomization; excluding them yields near-identical estimates.
“没有显著差异”不等于完全相同 / Null ≠ equality
Tutor 考试点估计 −0.004、SE 0.013，说明在本研究精度下无法检测到差异；它不是数学意义上的“证明完全无效应”。Tutor’s −0.004 estimate (SE 0.013) is statistically indistinguishable from zero at this precision; it does not prove exact equality.

08 / DESIGN

如果目标是学习，摩擦就是功能If learning matters, friction is a feature

产品不能只优化“完成率”或“当下正确率”。它必须保留生成性努力，并在撤掉工具后测量独立迁移。Do not optimize only completion or assisted accuracy. Preserve generative effort and test transfer after the tool is removed.

01
先作答，再求助 / Attempt before assistance先捕捉学生思路、置信度与卡点。Elicit work, confidence, and the point of confusion.
02
最小充分提示 / Minimal sufficient hint把答案拆成提示阶梯；挣扎后才升级信息。Escalate help only after productive struggle.
03
验证知识底座 / Verified grounding使用教师答案、评分标准与常见误区，降低幻觉反馈。Ground feedback in solutions, rubrics, and misconceptions.
04
把“解释”变成验收条件 / Require explanation即使答案正确，也要求学生说明为什么。A correct answer is not done until reasoning is articulated.
05
双指标看板 / Dual-metric dashboard同时追踪带辅助表现与无辅助迁移，警惕“借来的进步”。Track assisted performance and independent transfer together.
06
周期性撤掉 AI / Withdrawal tests用延迟、无工具、变式题检验是否真正掌握。Use delayed, tool-free, variant problems to measure retention.

真正的北极星不是“AI 使用量”The north star is not AI usage

理想产品位于右上角：有 AI 时表现更好，撤掉 AI 后也更强。本研究中 Tutor 向右走得很远，却没有向上；Base 向右、同时向下。The ideal system improves both assisted performance and independent transfer. Here, Tutor moves strongly right but not up; Base moves right and down.
