多模态能力正从识别内容，走向建立持久、可预测的世界表征。Multimodality is moving from recognition toward persistent, predictive representations of the world.

03

下一代计算不只等待点击；它理解意图、环境和连续上下文。The next computing layer will not wait for clicks; it will understand intent, context, and continuity.

THE SHAPE OF A CORE MEMBER

你既能把问题想深，
也愿意把手弄脏。You think deeply—
and still get your hands dirty.

01

有可验证的锋芒Proof over pedigree

论文、代码、产品、实验或一次漂亮的失败，都比头衔更有说服力。A paper, repo, product, experiment, or well-examined failure says more than a title.

02

能跨越研究与工程Cross the research–engineering gap

既尊重科学问题，也理解真实设备、用户与交付节奏的摩擦。Respect the science while understanding the friction of devices, users, and shipping.

03

把不确定性变成实验Turn uncertainty into experiments

不等完整地图；定义最小实验，用证据让下一步变清楚。Do not wait for a complete map. Design the smallest test that makes the next move obvious.

04

有创始人心态Operate like a founder

主动补位、坦诚争论、对结果负责，并愿意为长期价值做难而正确的事。Fill gaps, debate honestly, own outcomes, and choose hard long-term value over easy optics.
